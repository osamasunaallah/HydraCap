The problem
Pills and water are carried separately, so pills are easy to forget, and the existing fixes are bulky, leak-prone or fit only one bottle.

Compact & light

Users want something that adds almost nothing to the bottle they already carry.

Universal fit

It has to work with standard bottle necks, not one special bottle.

Affordable

A low-cost accessory that students, athletes, travellers and elderly users can all adopt.

Iterations

From a round well to a better-packed square.

Each 3D-printed prototype taught something. The round wells let pills cluster at the curved edges, so the next iteration moved to a rectangular well that lines capsules up and stores more.

What's next

Toward a production-ready product.

Learned

What the prototypes showed

  • Capacity matched the analytical model: 3–5 capsules
  • Early hinge misalignment was found and fixed
  • Cord holes cost internal space, so they were moved
  • Waterproof sealing was outside this stage's scope
Next

Future improvements

  • Dividers to separate different medications or doses
  • TPU or food-grade silicone for durability
  • A living or reinforced hinge for many open cycles
  • Water, impact and heat testing, then larger user tests
